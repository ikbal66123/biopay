                • Simulated: identity matching (every face resolves to the demo user), liveness depth sensing, palm-vein reading, balances, payments and settlement.Simulasi: pencocokan identitas (semua wajah dianggap pengguna demo), sensor kedalaman liveness, pembacaan vena telapak, saldo, pembayaran, dan settlement.
