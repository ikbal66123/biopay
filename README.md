# BioPay

**Pay with your face and palm. No card, no phone.**

BioPay is a two-factor biometric checkout for Southeast Asia. A shopper looks at a terminal, shows a palm, and the payment settles from the e-wallet or bank account they already use. Merchants can charge customers from their own phone, and consumer apps (for example travel booking) can add face-and-palm checkout.

This repository is an interactive prototype that runs entirely in the browser, as a single `index.html`.

**Live demo:** https://ikbal66123.github.io/biopay/ (open in Chrome, Edge or Safari and allow camera access)

**Video (3.5 min, Indonesian narration and on-screen text; covers store checkout, cardless ATM withdrawal, use cases, sign-up & security, and the bridge model):** [`video/biopay-demo.mp4`](video/biopay-demo.mp4) · interactive version: [`video/biopay-demo.html`](video/biopay-demo.html) · voice-over script: [`video/naskah-voiceover.md`](video/naskah-voiceover.md)

## What to try (about 1 minute each)

| | Who | Flow |
|---|---|---|
| **POV 1** | Shopper at a store | BioPay terminal: face scan, then palm scan, then a receipt. Foreign merchants trigger automatic FX conversion and a tourist VAT-refund receipt. After payment, the dashboard shows wallets, history, a Bio-Pass travel credential, and family sharing with daily limits. |
| **POV 2** | Merchant | *Kasku* e-wallet (fictional): tap **BioPay → Tagih Pelanggan**, enter an amount, scan the customer's face and palm, and the money lands in the merchant's balance. Also: QRIS, top up, transfer, bill payments. |
| **POV 3** | Traveler | *Lintas* travel app (fictional): search flights, hotels, trains or attractions, fill in passenger details and add-ons, apply a promo code, then pay with **BioPay** (face + palm), the in-app balance, or a virtual account. You get an e-ticket with a booking code; orders can be cancelled with a refund. The same three BioPay merchant features as POV 2 are built in. |
| **POV 4** | Cash user | *Cardless ATM* (concept): scan face and palm with the live camera, land on your BioPay page on the ATM screen, pick a funding source (bank account or e-wallet), then withdraw, deposit or check balances. No card, no PIN. |

**Accounts:** on the terminal's start screen, **Daftar BioID** creates an account in four steps: details (nationality, ID document type and number validated per country, e.g. 16-digit NIK for an Indonesian KTP; mobile number with a country-code picker, stored in E.164 format; explicit biometric consent), a photo of the ID document (camera capture inside a card frame, or upload; resized before storage and checked on-device for a face photo), face enrollment and palm enrollment. It then issues a BioID; **Masuk** signs in with email or phone plus password, or with your face. The dashboard, Bio-Pass and its passport MRZ line follow the signed-in account, and **Pengaturan** has sign-out and *delete account & biometric data*. Demo account: `ikbal@biopay.id` / `biopay123`.

**QR (BioPay dashboard → *QR Scan & Bayar*):** three tabs. *Scan* reads a QR code from the camera (BarcodeDetector where available, otherwise jsQR): a real QRIS code shows the merchant name, city and fixed amount (EMVCo tags 59, 60, 54), a BioPay QR shows the recipient; the payment is confirmed with a face scan instead of a PIN. *QR Saya* is a real, scannable QR to receive money, optionally with an amount. *QR Bayar* is a one-time code for the cashier to scan, refreshed every 60 seconds. QR codes generated by BioPay use a `BIOPAY:` payload, not a QRIS payload, so other apps don't try to pay a fictional merchant. Every QR payment and receipt lands in *Keuangan* automatically. The same three QR modes are in Kasku and Lintas (BioPay button → *QR*), paid from the app's own balance; there, *QR Bayar* creates a new single-use code on every tap, and a built-in “cashier phone” panel lets you play the other side: scan, type the amount, and the balance drops by exactly that amount (a used code is refused).

**Smart Route:** on the payment screen BioPay lists every linked wallet and bank and picks the best one automatically (enough balance, plus that wallet's promo: cashback, double points or no FX fee for foreign merchants). The shopper can override it. This is the "bridge" model in action: BioPay holds no money, it routes each payment to the wallet the customer already uses.

**Rewards, insights and security (BioPay dashboard):** *Hadiah* has BioPoints with tiers, a weekly streak, missions (e.g. pay 3 times, pay a bill, add a family member), vouchers to redeem and an invite code. *Insight* shows monthly spending by category and by funding source against an editable budget. *Pengaturan → Pusat keamanan* can lock the BioID in one tap, set a per-payment limit and a night-time limit, and lists recent activity; the payment screen enforces all three.

**Money management with an assistant (BioPay dashboard → *Keuangan*):** monthly income, spending and what is left; budgets per category (green, orange near the limit, red when over); savings pockets with progress; upcoming bills; and a chat assistant. Type plain Indonesian such as `makan siang 35rb`, `bonus freelance 1,2jt`, `nabung liburan 200rb`, `buat kantong laptop 8jt`, `sisa anggaran?`, `tagihan bulan ini?`, `tips hemat` or `hapus catatan terakhir`: it records the entry, picks the category, updates the budgets and answers from your data. Every BioPay transaction (payments, transfers, cash withdrawals, bills, top-ups) lands in *Keuangan* automatically with its date and time, already categorised. **Voice:** tap *Dengarkan “BioPay”* and say “BioPay, kopi dua ribu”: the wake word is detected, the amount is understood even in words (“dua puluh lima ribu”), the entry is recorded with the time and the assistant answers aloud; speech without the wake word is ignored. Voice uses the browser's Web Speech API (Chrome/Edge; Chrome processes the audio on Google's servers) and only listens while the page is open. While listening, the page keeps the screen awake (Screen Wake Lock), because browsers cut the microphone when the screen sleeps. In the native app the wake word keeps working while the phone sleeps: on Android through a foreground service with an on-device wake-word engine (e.g. Porcupine or openWakeWord, with the required “listening” notification); on iPhone, where only Siri may listen in the background, through Siri App Intents (“Hey Siri, BioPay catat kopi 2000”). The assistant in this prototype is rule-based and runs entirely in the browser (no data leaves the device); a production version could use a language model for free-form questions.

**For partners:** the landing page has a section for e-wallets, banks and PayLater providers: why integrate, a revenue simulator, a simulated API sandbox that shows the request, the `charge.authorize` webhook and the result, and the three-step integration with sample webhook code (concept API, not a live service).

**Language:** the terminal's start screen has an Indonesian / English / Japanese switch that translates the BioPay app (start, sign-up, sign-in, payment flow and dashboard); each BioPay instance keeps its own language.

**Bills in notifications:** Kasku and Lintas each have a bell with a badge for unpaid bills (electricity, internet, BPJS; hotel balance, PayLater). A bill can be paid with **QRIS** (dynamic QR with a 15-minute timer), **BioPay** (live face + palm scan) or **bank transfer** (pick BCA, BNI, BRI, Mandiri, CIMB Niaga, Permata or BSI to get a virtual account with instructions). Paid bills show a receipt with a reference number and land in the app's history; *Simulasikan tagihan baru masuk* adds a new bill for demos.

The landing section at the top of the page has **Try it** buttons that jump straight into each flow. The apps' interface is in Bahasa Indonesia, the launch market. The landing section switches between English and Indonesian.

## What is real and what is simulated

| Real | Simulated |
|---|---|
| Live camera feed on every scan screen | Identity matching: every face resolves to the demo user |
| On-device detection that a face or a hand is actually in front of the camera ([MediaPipe Tasks Vision](https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_detector)); the scan only passes once it is seen steadily for about 1.5 s | 3D liveness depth sensing and palm-vein reading |
| All UI flows, validation, state, and calculations (FX, VAT, promos, refunds, Smart Route choice, points, budgets, assistant parsing) | Balances, payments, settlement, bookings, wallet promos, the partner API and the ATM |
| Sign-up and sign-in with validation; passwords stored as salted SHA-256 hashes | Account storage: `localStorage` in this browser instead of a server |

No image or biometric data leaves the device. If the camera is blocked, or the detection model cannot load, scans fall back to a clearly labelled simulation mode so the demo never gets stuck.

Kasku and Lintas are fictional brands. DANA, GoPay, BCA, Agoda and other names appear only to illustrate integrations; there is no affiliation.

## Run it locally

Open `index.html` directly in Chrome or Edge, or serve the folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit http://localhost:8000. Browsers only allow camera access on `https://`, `localhost` or local files. An internet connection is needed for Tailwind CSS, fonts, icons and the detection models (all loaded from CDNs).

## How it is built

A single HTML file with plain JavaScript, Tailwind CSS (CDN), Phosphor icons and IBM Plex type. The main pieces:

- **`createBioPay(suffix, pov, ns)`**: the whole BioPay terminal app as a factory. It runs three times: on the POV 1 terminal and inside the POV 2 and POV 3 apps. The copies are generated from the POV 1 markup at load time (IDs get a suffix and handlers point at the right instance), so all three always look and behave the same.
- **`createMerchantKit(...)`**: the merchant features (charge a customer, show QRIS, open BioPay), shared by POV 2 and POV 3 in the same way.
- **`TV`**: the Lintas travel app (search, results, checkout, payment, e-tickets, orders, account).
- **`requestCameraAccess` / `scanBiometric`**: one shared camera stream per facing mode (permission asked once, camera released 6 s after the last scan) and MediaPipe face / hand detection with an overlay and a demo-mode fallback.

## Roadmap after the prototype

1. 1:N face identification against enrolled BioIDs, with certified presentation-attack detection.
2. Palm-vein capture on dedicated terminal hardware (near-infrared), with palm-print as the phone fallback.
3. Wallet and bank integrations through the providers' merchant APIs, and QRIS interoperability.
4. Consent, enrollment and data-protection flows that comply with Indonesia's personal data protection law (UU PDP).
